The idea behind the prompts

What is this?

150

One hundred and fifty words

Long enough for a turn, an image, or a whole tiny world. Short enough to make every sentence earn its place. A prompt is only the beginning; the shape and meaning of the story are yours. The prompt itself is part of the story, so its words count toward the 150-word total.

01

Start with one sentence

You do not need to master compression, layered meaning, or a clever final turn before you begin. Continue the prompt and finish a story in 150 words. That is enough. With practice, you will discover which details matter, how one sentence can do several jobs, and what your own kind of nuance looks like.

With love to Glipho 150

Prompt 150 carries forward a practice I loved on Glipho 150, a service that is no longer with us. Its brief beginnings became years of stories and showed me how much possibility can live in a handful of words. This site is made in fond remembrance, and in the hope that the invitation remains: take these few words and make them yours.
