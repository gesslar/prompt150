The idea behind the prompts

What is this?

150

One hundred and fifty words

Long enough for a turn, an image, or a whole tiny world. Short enough to make every sentence earn its place. A prompt is only the beginning; the shape and meaning of the story are yours. The prompt itself is part of the story, so its words count toward the 150-word total.
